WELCOME TO PUNAKHA DZONG

Discover the history, beauty and cultural importance of one of Bhutan's most famous dzongs.


01. Introduction

Punakha Dzong is one of the most beautiful and historic dzongs in Bhutan. It is also known as Pungthang Dewa Chenpoi Phodrang, meaning the Palace of Great Happiness. It stands between the Pho Chhu and Mo Chhu rivers.

02. History

Punakha Dzong was built in 1637 by Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal. It played an important role in the history and unification of Bhutan. It served as the capital of Bhutan until 1955. The first King of Bhutan was crowned here in 1907.

03. Importance

Punakha Dzong is an important religious, cultural and historical site. It serves as the winter residence of the Central Monastic Body. Its beautiful courtyards, traditional architecture and wooden carvings attract many visitors.

04. Natural Beauty

The dzong is located at the meeting point of the Pho Chhu and Mo Chhu rivers. Its beautiful surroundings, green landscapes and purple jacaranda flowers make it a special place to visit.
